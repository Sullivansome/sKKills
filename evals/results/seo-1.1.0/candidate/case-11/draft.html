Help a new customer take the first step

Use these two onboarding email templates to introduce one useful setup task, then offer a gentle reminder. Replace every bracketed placeholder before sending, and remove any instruction that does not match your actual product.

SetupLeaf is a fictional tool for scheduled onboarding emails. The only verified workflow for this draft is importing a contact list and scheduling an email. These examples illustrate that workflow; they are not customer results or evidence of a live service.

1. Welcome and a clear first task

Illustrative email: use when introducing a customer to their first scheduled message.

Subject: [First name], plan your first onboarding email

Hi [First name],

Welcome to [Your team or product name]. Start with one message: what should a new customer do first?

Write that step in a short email. For this SetupLeaf example, import your contact list and schedule the email for [chosen date and time]. Check your recipients and the message text before scheduling.

Your first task: replace this email's placeholders and choose a send time that fits your audience.

Thanks,
[Sender name]

Customize: Replace the greeting and sender, name one actual first task, and specify the intended send date, time and timezone. Use a contact list you are authorized to email. The date is your scheduling choice, not a recommendation inferred from performance data.

2. A gentle first-step reminder

Illustrative email: a general reminder suitable for a manually chosen schedule. It does not assume behavior tracking.

Subject: A quick check before your first onboarding email

Hi [First name],

If your first email is still on your to-do list, keep it small: one next step and a clear reason to take it.

Review your contact list and replace any remaining placeholders. Then schedule your email for [chosen date and time]. If you have already scheduled it, you can skip this reminder.

Before you finish, read the message as a new customer: is the next step clear?

Thanks,
[Sender name]

Customize: Choose a reminder interval deliberately; no optimal interval has been tested here. Adapt the task to the customer's context. Schedule this as a separate email if appropriate; this example does not establish automatic triggers or completion-based suppression.
