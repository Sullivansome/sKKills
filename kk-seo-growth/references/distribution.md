# Distribution and credible coverage

## Match audience and channel

Identify where intended users learn, compare, and buy. Consider relevant search, videos, communities, reviewers, newsletters, stores, and existing users as evidence permits. Do not assume Reddit or any platform fits every product.

Turn a useful result into a demonstration and substantive destination. A tutorial should complete a real task; a game clip should show actual play; a downloadable resource should work independently of its promotion.

Use product-specific links and campaign parameters where supported without sensitive data. Measure useful outcomes. Distinguish social traffic to an SEO page from organic search acquisition.

## Prospects

Record outlet/page, audience fit, relevant work, verified editorial contact, proposed value, evidence, and status. Do not guess emails or collect unnecessary personal information.

Prioritize relevance and editorial usefulness over domain scores alone. Small publishers can be valuable, but do not promise equivalent conversion or durability to established media.

Offer a real test, original data, example, or sufficient product access for an honest review. Disclose ownership/incentives. Do not demand praise, a ranking position, a link passing ranking credit, or undisclosed endorsement.

## Outreach

Draft concise individualized messages with one clear offer and room for refusal. Use recipient-resolution capabilities when applicable.

Sending requires explicit user authorization. Importing into an active campaign, activating sequences, replying, and initial sending are external actions. A draft-only task never authorizes them. Reuse existing explicit authorization without repeatedly asking.

Before requesting missing approval, complete the recipient list and exact messages. Respect opt-outs and avoid duplicate contact. Do not adopt lookalike domains, warm-up promises, daily-volume rules, or unsolicited sequences merely because an article recommends them.

## Communities and reviews

Use genuine accounts and useful participation under community rules. Disclose affiliation. Do not impersonate customers, fabricate social proof, or plant comments solely to influence AI citations.

Invite real users to leave honest reviews through the appropriate workflow. Do not condition benefits on positive feedback or present manufactured praise as independent evidence. Verify applicable platform rules before implementation.

## Assets and partnerships

Publish documents, repositories, videos, or other assets because they help users. Do not promise ranking preference from hosting on a large platform. Public sharing, discovery, indexing, ranking, and referral conversion are separate steps.

For sponsorships, agree on accurate claims, disclosure, and appropriate link qualification. Evaluate audience fit and outcomes rather than purchasing ranking credit.

Adapt strong material across formats. Avoid bulk identical posting across many domains as a substitute for relevance.
