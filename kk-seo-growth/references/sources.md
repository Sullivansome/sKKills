- [Title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link): descriptive titles and display limits.

- [Faceted navigation](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation): URL combinations, crawl controls, empty/invalid results.
- [Using generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content): useful content and scaled-content abuse boundaries.
